Walking Free Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Walking Free is a sobriety and recovery companion designed to provide practical recovery tools while respecting the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Walking Free application.

Information You Provide

Walking Free may allow you to enter information such as recovery progress, reflections, sponsor or recovery contact information, reminders, meeting information, and other information you choose to use within the app.

Walking Free does not sell your personal information.

Sign in with Apple

Walking Free supports Sign in with Apple. When you use Sign in with Apple, Apple may provide Walking Free with information necessary to identify your account, subject to the privacy choices you make with Apple.

Apple may allow you to hide your email address by providing a private relay email address instead.

Recovery Information

Information you enter into Walking Free for your personal recovery experience is intended to provide the app's recovery features. Walking Free does not sell recovery information to advertisers or data brokers.

In-App Purchases

Walking Free offers optional one-time support purchases. Purchases made through the iOS application are processed by Apple through the App Store.

Walking Free does not directly receive or store your credit card or other payment card information.

Third-Party Services

Walking Free may rely on platform or third-party services necessary to provide certain app functionality. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information associated with Walking Free. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Walking Free is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Walking Free, please contact us so the matter can be addressed.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Walking Free changes. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
